ONE TURN != ONE ROUND                               
                                                    
A TURN is one complete input to the next.           
Every round and tool call in between                
belongs to that one turn.                           
                                                    
  turns=1  (61 request(s), 64 tool call(s))         
  rounds 1-61   calls=64   rows=4890                
  prompt="README 30s ..."
