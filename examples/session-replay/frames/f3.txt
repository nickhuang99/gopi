SCREEN REPLAY - BYTE-EXACT                          
                                                    
screen-replay writes a recording's render           
bytes back out, unchanged. Step frame by            
frame, or compare two recordings:                   
                                                    
  gopi screen-replay <id> --step                    
  gopi screen-replay <id> --verify A B              
                                                    
  A: frames=142 bytes=11092                         
  B: frames=43  bytes=1237
