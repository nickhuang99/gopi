FAILURE IS AN ATTEMPT, NOT THE WORK                 
                                                    
A worker can finish all its work and still          
end 'failed' - e.g. the delivery push at            
the END hook is refused. The commit stays           
safe in that row's own clone.                       
                                                    
  gopi task status <id>                             
  status: failed   resumable: yes                   
  holder: (none)   run_lock: free
